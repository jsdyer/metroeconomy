Reading the comparison

Each month averages the survey estimates for that month and the two before it. Consecutive points share two months. A year-over-year comparison uses the same three months one year earlier.

These are seasonally adjusted estimates for a metropolitan area, including its surrounding communities. Small changes can reflect sampling variability. Statistics Canada can revise earlier estimates.
